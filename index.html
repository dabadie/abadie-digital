01

What I work on

Two decades on one question: how can the state deliver services people actually want to use?

DPI

Digital Public Infrastructure

Neutral, pro bono architecture advice for national DPI programmes: digital identity, payments, data exchange and verifiable credentials. With CDPI's DPI-as-a-Packaged-Solution, deployment takes months instead of years.

AI

AI for the public sector

Lead author of the DPI-AI Framework. Its core claim is that government AI works best as interoperable blocks on top of strong DPI rails, not as a vertical silo tied to one vendor.

GOV

Digital government delivery

Running digital teams like a civic-tech startup inside government: user needs first, in-house teams, open source, and shipping. The principle I keep coming back to: doing beats everything.

The journey

From running a city's call centre to shaping how countries adopt DPI and AI.

  1. 2024 — now

That covers neutral architecture advice, DPI-as-a-Packaged-Solution (DaaS), and bringing together governments, open-source communities, private innovators and funders. I joined as Senior Technical Advisor for Latin America and the Caribbean in early 2024.

    • DPI-AI Framework

  2. 2023 — 2024

    Banco Macro

    Digital Transformation Advisor to the CEO

    Designed the bank's GovTech division and a citizen app, and built a blockchain wallet for governments to pay social benefits. Also designed the Knowledge Economy division.

  3. 2020 — 2023

    Public Digital London · global

    Latin America & Caribbean lead

    Engagement lead for every Spanish-speaking project. Clients included the IDB, CAF, UNDP and the governments of Belize, Panama, Jamaica, Peru, Trinidad and Tobago, Buenos Aires City, the State of California and British Columbia, as well as Bloomberg Philanthropies. I wrote the IDB Data Strategy Playbook and built an executive programme in digital transformation for IE University.

  4. 2015 — 2019

    Government of Argentina Presidency of the Nation

    Undersecretary of Digital Government

    Argentina's first Chief Digital Officer. I advised the President on the digital economy and led the 2018 G20 discussion on government transformation. The team ran like a civic-tech startup at the heart of government, and shipped:

    • Decree 87/2017: wrote the presidential decree that created the National Public Sector Digital Platform (websites, apps, the procedures guide, SMS, phone and in-person service, social media) and established Mi Argentina as every citizen's digital profile.
    • Resolution 139/2018: brought AI virtual assistants into public service. It created the government-wide automated virtual assistance service for citizens and gave my team the mandate to build and coordinate virtual assistants across all national agencies.
    • Disposition 24/2019: signed the rule that made github.com/argob the government's official open-source repository and released the code of Mi Argentina, its ID service, the API gateway and the Poncho design system.
    • Mi Argentina: the citizen platform and digital wallet, with 3M+ users by 2019 and more than 30M today, holding legally valid ID, the country's first digital driving licence, vehicle papers and vaccination records.
    • argentina.gob.ar: 1,000+ websites merged into one domain; 600 editors, 50,000 pages, 127M users in four years.
    • Chat Crecer: a virtual assistant for pregnancy and early childhood that increased prenatal check-ups. It won the 2019 Webby Award for Health.
    • National Digital Inclusion Plan: 250 trainers taught 400,000+ people and 16,000 small businesses and cooperatives in 160 cities to use the internet.
  5. 2011 · 2015

    Mauricio Macri campaigns PRO

    Head of Technology, 2015 presidential campaign · Digital team, 2011 Buenos Aires mayoral campaign

    In 2015 I ran the national campaign's technology infrastructure and digital product development, reporting to the Director of Digital Communication. In 2011 I worked on digital strategy and applications for the re-election campaign in Buenos Aires.

  6. 2007 — 2015

    Government of the City of Buenos Aires

    Head of digital · Electronic Government Director · Head of Web · CTO Citizen Services

    I led a 40-person in-house team of developers, UX designers, civic hackers and geographers, working with Lean Startup methods and open source.

    • Mi BA (2015): a personal account for every resident to see their information and services in one place. It was the genesis of Mi Argentina.
    • Services closer to home: moved in-person procedures out of central offices into the city's 15 comunas (formerly CGPs), with online appointment booking.
    • 147: merged 28 phone lines into a single citizen contact centre, the city's 311, backed by an Open311-based service request system.
    • buenosaires.gob.ar: consolidated 400+ websites into one; 22M visits a year by 2011.
    • BA Cómo Llego: the city's journey planner app, named App of the Year in 2014.
  7. 2000 — 2007

    Private sector

    General Manager, PackagingBrokers.com · CIO, Mailparking.com · IT Manager, LaCasilla.com.ar

    Early internet businesses in Buenos Aires and Curitiba. This is where I learned to build products people pay for.

Questions

Short answers about my work on AI and Digital Public Infrastructure for the public sector.

Who is Daniel Abadie?

Daniel Abadie is an Argentine expert on AI for the public sector and Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI). He is Head of Technology & Partnerships at the Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI) and lead author of the DPI-AI Framework. From 2015 to 2019 he was Argentina's Undersecretary of Digital Government, where he created Mi Argentina, now used by more than 30 million people. Apolitical named him one of the 20 most influential people in digital government.

What is Daniel Abadie's view on AI in government?

He argues that AI in government works best as interoperable AI Blocks running on top of strong DPI rails such as digital identity, payments and data exchange, and orchestrated through public workflows, rather than as standalone systems tied to one vendor. Without solid DPI, AI cannot work reliably inside government.

What is the DPI-AI Framework?

The DPI-AI Framework is a 2026 vision paper and toolkit from CDPI, with Daniel Abadie as lead author, on building AI-ready nations through Digital Public Infrastructure. It defines AI Blocks, DPI Workflows and Public Agents, and includes a 9-step implementation guide. Reviewers came from Mozilla, the Gates Foundation, UCL, OpenFn, AWS and Public Digital.

What has Daniel Abadie built?

Mi Argentina, the national citizen platform and digital wallet with 30M+ users; argentina.gob.ar, which merged 1,000+ government websites; Chat Crecer, a virtual assistant for pregnancy and early childhood that won a 2019 Webby Award; Mi BA and the 147 contact centre in Buenos Aires; and Argentina's National Digital Inclusion Plan, which trained 400,000+ people.
